# Other reading practice audit

The review covers all four supplied files as separate collections: Reading Tests
1_Teacher (24 readings), 2_Teacher (15), 3_Teacher (10), and 3_Student (13).
The two files numbered 3 contain different passages. In total, 62 readings and
620 questions were checked against their supplied passages and keys.

The collection is explicitly labelled as not aligned with the B1 assessment
level. Difficulty may be above or below the B1 assessments. No equivalence to a
formal examination is asserted.

## Other item repairs

1_Teacher R1 Q10, R2 Q4, R5 Q7 and R8 Q10 had competing or overlapping true
choices. The affected distractor was narrowed or replaced while retaining the
intended construct and key. R11 Q10 now asks directly about continuous air-quality
measurement rather than offering both “often” and “all the time.” R17 Q9 now says
“almost one-fifth,” matching the source’s “almost 20%.” R19 Q5’s transport choice
now matches the source’s scope of public transport.

1_Teacher R3 Q3 was a broken image item: extracting the embedded bottle pictures
lost the Word arrow overlays, while full Word rendering displaced one answer
picture over the passage. It now asks the meaning of *rim*, with the keyed answer
“top edge around the opening.” The passage images are made from clean excerpts
that exclude question rows and their anchored drawings. The other four picture
items were visually checked: matchbox D, hugging B, road vehicle C and anchor B.
The food-transport item now specifies the road vehicle, so a plane is not a competing
answer from the same passage.

1_Teacher R3 Q10’s double “to” is removed. R9 Q6 explicitly asks for the passage’s
description of a seamount. R11 Q4 uses the source’s “traffic emissions” instead of
narrowing it to cars. R13 Q4 restores the missing word “phone,” and Q7 uses “fixed
and definite” for *concrete*. R14 Q6 clarifies *out-of-season produce*. R15 Q7 asks
what the passage actually states about home schooling, without assuming that the
parents must be the teachers.

2_Teacher R8 Q2’s circular answer (“the two basic ingredients”) is replaced with
the actual ingredients, flour and water. Other keys in this file were supported
by their passages. The reading on map scale expects some prior vocabulary knowledge;
that is retained as a difficulty consideration for this non-B1 collection.

3_Teacher R4 Q6’s falling-orbit hypothetical now states that the thrusters do not
restore the orbit. R10 Q7 identifies the 2018 Robin Hood film rather than leaving
the title indistinguishable from the fictional character.

3_Student R4 Q7 no longer treats the weaker statement “sometimes” as incompatible
with a constant risk of famine; the revised choice says shortages would occur
only rarely. R5 Q5 correctly defines a phreatic explosion as resulting from heated
water, rather than reversing the cause and effect. R5 Q9 avoids the unsupported
absolute “nobody.” R8 Q10 removes two competing inferences. R12 Q4 replaces the
overlapping distractor “light” with a different referent. R13 Q10 makes clear that
most, not all, migrants found little or no gold. R3 Q9’s duplicate wrong choices
“cost less” and “cheaper” are no longer identical.

## Source formatting and line references

Passage excerpts preserve the source text layout, printed line-number column,
headings, and illustrations. The Word files are converted from passage-only
copies, so teacher keys and question options cannot accidentally enter the
passage-image panel. Browser text uses the same printed markers and retains
line boundaries while permitting wrapping on small screens.

The erroneous gap references in 1_Teacher R3 and R14, 2_Teacher R1 and R2,
3_Teacher R5, and 3_Student R9 are corrected in the rendered excerpts. Direct-word
references are matched to the positions in the rendered excerpt; the conversion
logs are retained as reading-line-reference-changes.json and
reading-multiline-reference-changes.json. Temperature typography such as 4°C is
restored in browser text and questions. Target words are italicized.

The first draft importer mistakenly included the first diabetes question in the
passage because its answer-label column was missing. The passage boundary
recognizer now handles that source irregularity. This error was caught before
the new collection was linked from the live home page.

The passages retain source-era statistics and some simplified or fictional
accounts. This is a review of internal passage support and question validity,
not an exhaustive factual or historical verification of every source claim.
