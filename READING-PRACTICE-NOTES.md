# Reading Practice Tests ("5 Student")

Source: supplied ENGL010_Supplemental Reading Tests 5_Student.docx.
Includes 18 readings, 172 questions, eight original picture choices, and 148
vocabulary entries from the individual reading lists at the end of the packet.
The original resource remains the source of the teaching content; this is an
interactive adaptation. No author name is asserted based solely on Word metadata.

Every reading has a 20-minute deadline persisted with its answers, randomized
choice order, and flags. Leaving the page does not pause the deadline. Expiry
locks and submits the attempt. Full retakes receive a new 20 minutes and keep
the first submitted score. Feedback appears after submission.

Vocabulary has separate untimed flashcards and a complete word list for each
reading, using the supplied words, parts of speech, CEFR labels, Arabic meanings,
and example sentences. Known-word status is saved on the device. The user's
B1 alignment note appears at the collection level; individual vocabulary labels
remain B1/B2 as supplied.

Print line references become paragraph references so questions remain usable on
responsive screens. Reading 8's location-answer choices become paragraph numbers.
Paragraph headings and sentence gaps are retained, and passage titles are shown
only after submission to avoid giving away best-title answers.

Reading 16 question 2 has a documented clarification: the original options A
and C were both defensible from the passage (piracy as old as sea trade; first
recorded example 3,400 years ago). The interactive question asks specifically
about the first recorded example, with C stating the Mediterranean 3,400 years
ago. Other supplied answer keys are retained. Scoring checks compare all items
with the imported key; they do not establish an independent full item audit.

Expanded skills, reading-test, and listening menus share the same pale green
container, white cards, border, radius, and responsive spacing.

Checks: imported question and vocabulary counts; four choices per item; all 172
keys graded correctly; eight images rendered; saved answer/flag/order/deadline;
automatic expiry after reload; immutable first score across retakes; flashcards,
Arabic display, word lists; desktop/mobile layout and menu switching.
