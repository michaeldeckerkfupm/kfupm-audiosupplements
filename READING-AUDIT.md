# Reading content and formatting audit

Reviewed: 28 October 2026.
Sources: Chris Molcher’s ENGL010 Reading Skills Guidebook (2026), and the supplied
ENGL010 Supplemental Reading Tests 5_Student Word packet.

The review covered all 12 skills, their worked examples, the 23 guide activities
with 42 scored questions, and all 18 packet readings with 172 questions. It also
checked the 148 per-reading vocabulary entries for import and presentation.
Question validity was checked against each displayed passage and image, rather
than by merely comparing the program with the supplied keys. This is a review
of passage support and assessment design, not an exhaustive independent fact
check of every historical, scientific, geographical, or numerical source claim.

## Conversion and formatting repairs

Skill 4’s two question types now have separate headings and explanatory paragraphs.
Its three sample question stems are separate bullets. Across the guide, definition
headings, strategy steps, tips, and worked-example choices are rendered with
headings, ordered lists, bullets, and separate answer sections. The content is
inserted as text, not interpreted as arbitrary HTML.

Twenty-two packet pronoun-reference items now quote the target clause or sentence
as well as naming its paragraph. Removing the original print line numbers had
left several possible instances of “it,” “which,” or “they” in the same paragraph.
This repair restores the intended target without requiring fixed-width print lines.

Reading 15’s three referenced diagrams were missing from the initial conversion.
They are now restored and labelled Diagram 1, Diagram 2, and Diagram 3. Reading
14’s internal headings are separated visually from the following prose. Negative
question wording is emphasized in both interactive sections.

Skill 2, Practice 2 now includes the writing-on-stone sentence from the packet,
so paragraph 2’s reference to “the solution” has an identifiable problem before it.
Skill 12’s worked example asks where the exact words “Tigris and Euphrates” appear
on line 3, avoiding an imprecise claim that the full phrase including “rivers” is
on that line. Its explanation acknowledges continuation onto line 4. The nonexistent
Line 6 distractor in Practice 1 Q2 has been replaced with the existing Line 4.

Mechanical repairs include “11th century,” complete May dates in the Eiffel Tower
passage, “visitors shopped,” subject–verb agreement in the weather passages,
“foundations are,” “you have to wait,” the missing verb in the London distractor,
km², date commas, and punctuation around “widower”/“lawyers.” The Capital Cities
passage’s description of Scotland as a city now names Edinburgh instead.

The reading collection title is now **B1 Reading Practice Tests**. Its subtitle is
**"Reading Tests 5_Student" - Aligned with Assessments**.

## Remaining editorial cautions

Several passages retain dated statistics, historical simplifications, and broad
claims from the supplied resource. In particular, the packet calls the United
Kingdom a grouping of three countries, while the guide separately describes four.
That inconsistency should be resolved in a separate factual content review rather
than treating a passage-comprehension key as verification of the claim.

Reading 15 Q4 and Q5 test closely related information about road safety. They remain
individually answerable but provide less independent coverage than two different
targets. Some other distractors are conspicuously implausible. These are assessment
quality limitations rather than incorrect keys and have not prompted wholesale rewriting.

The vocabulary list includes “expedition” under Reading 16 even though it is not
used in that passage; the source itself says “not in text but implied.” The entry
is retained as supplementary vocabulary rather than inventing a passage example.
Individual vocabulary CEFR labels remain B1/B2 as supplied; the collection-level
B1 label follows the user’s requested course positioning.

## Saved attempts and checks

Readings with changed scoring or answer content and guide activities with changed
choices receive a content revision key, so previously scored answers are not
silently treated as attempts at the revised version. Their unrelated vocabulary
progress and all unchanged readings keep their existing storage identifiers.

Browser checks exercise all reading keys, shuffled grading, image loading,
post-submission feedback, timer expiry, persistence, flags, retries, vocabulary,
menu switching, and mobile width. Separate checks verify the guide’s semantic
headings and worked-example lists, the restored diagram count, revised keys, and
explicit pronoun contexts. These runtime checks supplement the content review;
they do not by themselves demonstrate item validity.

Visual follow-up: the source Word drawing anchors caused two captions and the third driving diagram to overlap passage text in the rendered image. The corrected Reading 15 page keeps the original text positions and printed line numbers, moves the second caption into the clear space below its diagram, and places diagram 3 and its caption below the passage.
