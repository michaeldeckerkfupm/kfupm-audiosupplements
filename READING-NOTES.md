# Reading Skills Guidebook adaptation

Source: *ENGL010: Reading Skills Guidebook*, Chris Molcher, 2026 (54 pages).
The source cover credits Chris Molcher. That credit appears in the contents,
each skill lesson, and every practice passage. The interactive interface is an
adaptation, not a claim to authorship of the original guide.

The first of three reading cards opens a table of contents for all 12 skills.
The other two cards are reserved for later material. The guide preserves the
explanations, strategies, tips, worked examples, 23 practice passages, and all
42 multiple-choice practice items. The main-idea activities also retain the
three planning prompts as optional, unscored notes, with model answers after
submission.

The layout and interactions follow the earlier
`ENGL010-Reading-Practice-Netlify.zip` template: passage beside all questions,
randomized answer positions saved across visits, flag-and-return navigation,
feedback after submission, first-attempt scores, and retries for missed items.
These short skill activities are untimed rather than using the template's
20-minute full-reading-test timer. They are practice activities, not a claim
to reproduce the Examplify application or an official examination.

## Source adaptations

The Skill 7 worked example on page 33 gives a following-paragraph answer about
paper from China, but its short passage ends before providing that transition.
The interactive example adds the relevant papyrus-limitations and China
transition sentences from the guide's page 35 practice passage. This makes the
worked answer supported by the displayed text. The practice item keys remain
as supplied.

Pronoun-reference practice questions quote their target sentence instead of
using print line references that would change in a responsive layout. Skill 12
retains the original numbered source lines, including line boundaries when a
numbered line wraps on a small screen. Its explanations clarify sentences that
continue across source lines.

Practice explanations are adapted from the guide and checked against each
displayed passage. The source's practice choices and keyed answers are retained.

## Validation

Content validation checked 12 skills, 23 activities, 42 four-option items,
source keys, explanations, original line boundaries, and authorship labels.
Chromium checks traversed every skill and activity, answered every keyed item,
and verified grading, delayed feedback, flags, saved choices and order after
reload, saved planning notes, first-attempt scores after retries, reading and
listening navigation, outside-click collapse, and a 390px mobile viewport.
Desktop contents, lesson, activity, and mobile activity screenshots were
visually reviewed.

The introductory heading and sentence were removed from the home page.
The visible heading and browser title are now `PEP Supplemental Materials`;
the KFUPM logo remains unchanged.
